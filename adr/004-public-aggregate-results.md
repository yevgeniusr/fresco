# ADR-004 — Public aggregate experiment results

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-20

## Context

Open learning requires inspectable outcomes, while small experiments can expose individuals.

## Decision

Publish experiment specifications, aggregate measurements, limitations, and decisions. Keep individual measurements and participation records private.

## Alternatives

Fully private results undermine open learning; participant-level publication creates unacceptable privacy risk.

## Consequences

Moderators must check small-cohort re-identification risk before publication. Public pages never query or render individual measurements.
