# ADR-005 — Voluntary opt-in experiments only

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-20

## Context

Fresco intentionally changes environments and therefore can influence behavior.

## Decision

Participation requires explicit, informed opt-in after showing the intervention, collected data, privacy boundary, time burden, and withdrawal path. Covert assignment is forbidden.

## Alternatives

Automatic enrollment and opaque randomized exposure were rejected as violations of Human autonomy, Transparency, Voluntary experimentation, and Right to leave.

## Consequences

Recruitment may be slower. Trust and ethical legitimacy are treated as hard constraints, not conversion costs.
