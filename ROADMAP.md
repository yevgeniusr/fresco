# Roadmap

## v0.1 — One complete learning loop

Public method and experiment catalogue, citizen accounts, structured proposals, explicit experiment opt-in, discussion, aggregate results, community signals, OKF, RFCs, ADRs, and demo data.

## v0.2 — Operate a real founding cohort

- Moderator workspace and transparent transition log.
- Real baseline/result collection with withdrawal-aware aggregation.
- Email verification and password reset.
- Accessibility audit and participant safety review.
- Knowledge-promotion workflow from reviewed results to OKF.
- Research citations attached to hypotheses.

## Later, only after evidence

Domain modules for health, learning, social connection, contribution, resources, and physical environments; privacy-preserving measurement integrations; governance experiments; self-hosting improvements.

Not planned: tokens, reputation scores, surveillance, hidden nudging, a social feed, a city simulator, or engagement-maximizing mechanics.
