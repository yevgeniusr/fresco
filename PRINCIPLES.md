# Fresco Principles

Fresco designs environments to empower people, not to control them.

1. Human autonomy
2. Transparency
3. Voluntary experimentation
4. Privacy
5. Explainability
6. Right to leave
7. Scientific falsifiability
8. Open discussion
9. Pluralism
10. Minimize coercion

The canonical, reviewable definitions live in [`.okf/principles`](.okf/principles/). A principle can change, but never silently.
