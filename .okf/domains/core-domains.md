# Initial domains

- **Health:** movement, sleep, nutrition, preventive health, and healthy routines.
- **Learning:** tutoring, projects, skills, knowledge sharing, and mentorship.
- **Social:** friendship, activities, collaboration, and loneliness reduction.
- **Contribution:** community problems, projects, research, and volunteering.
- **Governance:** goals, ideas, experiments, results, decisions, and system changes.

Fresco v0.1 concentrates on the generic experiment loop rather than deep domain modules.
