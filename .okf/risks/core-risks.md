# Initial risk register

- **RISK-001 — Metric gaming:** proxies displace the human outcome they were meant to represent.
- **RISK-002 — Hidden coercion:** defaults, social pressure, or withheld access make nominally voluntary systems coercive.
- **RISK-003 — Engagement substitution:** app activity is optimized instead of wellbeing.
- **RISK-004 — Privacy leakage:** small cohorts or comments expose participant-level behavior.
- **RISK-005 — Correlation as causation:** small uncontrolled experiments are overstated.
- **RISK-006 — Majority harm:** popular preferences reduce access or dignity for minorities.
- **RISK-007 — Small-sample overconfidence:** uncertainty is hidden behind precise-looking data.

Every experiment must name relevant risks, guardrails, and a stop condition.
