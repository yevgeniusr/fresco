# Initial metric catalogue

## METRIC-001 — Weekly active minutes

- **Definition:** minutes of intentional or naturally occurring moderate physical activity per consenting participant per seven-day period.
- **Unit:** minutes/week.
- **Method:** participant self-report in v0.1.
- **Limitations:** recall and social-desirability bias; not a clinical measure.

## METRIC-002 — Voluntary learning minutes

- **Definition:** minutes a participant intentionally spends learning without employer, school, or Fresco compulsion.
- **Unit:** minutes/day.
- **Method:** participant self-report.
- **Limitations:** learning quality and attention are not captured by duration.

## METRIC-003 — Meaningful social interactions

- **Definition:** participant-identified interactions that involved mutual attention, support, collaboration, or substantive conversation.
- **Unit:** interactions/week.
- **Method:** weekly self-report.
- **Limitations:** subjective, culturally variable, and easy to misinterpret.

## METRIC-004 — Experiment retention

- **Definition:** opted-in participants who remain through the planned end, excluding explicit safety cancellations.
- **Unit:** percentage.
- **Method:** aggregate participation state.
- **Limitations:** retention is not proof of benefit and must never become the ultimate objective.

## METRIC-005 — Self-reported enjoyment

- **Definition:** participant response to a clearly worded enjoyment prompt.
- **Unit:** percentage change or 1–5 rating.
- **Method:** voluntary survey.
- **Limitations:** subjective and vulnerable to response bias.
