# The Fresco Method

Fresco is not a finished model for society. It is a process for discovering better ones.

```text
OBSERVE
   ↓
DEFINE DESIRED OUTCOME
   ↓
FORM HYPOTHESIS
   ↓
DESIGN ENVIRONMENTAL CHANGE
   ↓
DEFINE METRICS + GUARDRAILS
   ↓
RUN SMALL EXPERIMENT
   ↓
MEASURE
   ↓
REVIEW
   ↓
SCALE / MODIFY / REPEAT / STOP / INCONCLUSIVE
   ↓
UPDATE KNOWLEDGE
   ↓
REPEAT
```

## Fresco Experiment Spec

Every meaningful experiment states:

- **Observation:** what is happening now?
- **Goal:** what human outcome should improve?
- **Hypothesis:** which environmental change might improve it?
- **Intervention:** what changes, exactly?
- **Participants:** who can join, and how is voluntary consent preserved?
- **Baseline:** what happens before the intervention?
- **Primary metric:** which single measure most directly tests the hypothesis?
- **Secondary metrics:** what adds context?
- **Guardrails:** what must not get worse?
- **Duration:** how long does the test run?
- **Data collection:** what is collected and why?
- **Privacy:** what is public and what stays private?
- **Result:** what happened?
- **Limitations:** why may the result mislead?
- **Decision:** Scale, Modify, Repeat, Stop, or Inconclusive.

The method can be used without Fresco software. The software makes the method visible, repeatable, and easier to inspect.
